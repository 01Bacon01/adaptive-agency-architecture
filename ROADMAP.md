## Research Philosophy

The objective of Adaptive Agency Architecture is not to maximize complexity.

The objective is to discover the smallest set of primitives capable of describing adaptive systems across multiple domains while remaining interpretable, extensible, and experimentally testable.

Every version should move toward greater conceptual simplicity and greater empirical usefulness.
